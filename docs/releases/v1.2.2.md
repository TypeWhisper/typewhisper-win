# TypeWhisper v1.2.2

TypeWhisper 1.2.2 lets you dictate into the World of Warcraft chat and makes pasting into
Flutter apps work. The
[1.2.0 release notes](https://github.com/TypeWhisper/typewhisper-win/releases/tag/v1.2.0)
describe what is new in 1.2, including managed settings for organizations and the
machine-wide MSI.

## Dictate into the World of Warcraft chat

- Turn on **World of Warcraft** under **Settings › Advanced › Integrations** and click
  **Install add-on**. TypeWhisper finds the game through Battle.net and installs the
  TypeWhisper add-on into every version on the PC: Retail, Classic and Forever.
- While the game is in front, your dictation shortcut opens the chat, inserts the text
  and sends it. A chat that is already open stays open with its text. Long text goes out
  as several messages of up to 255 bytes, and a message never starts with a slash, so a
  dictation cannot run a game command.
- The add-on shows the dictation as a cast bar in the game's style: a waveform while you
  speak, and a bar that fills while TypeWhisper transcribes.
- Sending, the cast bar and an optional translation of your messages are set on the
  add-on's page in the game: type `/tw`. Translation uses the default LLM of your
  workflows. TypeWhisper reads these settings when you type `/reload` or log out.
- The add-on is also available on its own from
  [TypeWhisper/typewhisper-wow](https://github.com/TypeWhisper/typewhisper-wow).

## Fixes

- Pasting into apps built with Flutter works. TypeWhisper now sends the keys of a paste,
  of the Enter after it and of selected-text capture with the scan codes a real keyboard
  sends, so Flutter no longer receives a plain "v" instead of Ctrl+V. (#732)

## Updating

- 1.2.0 and 1.2.1 installations are offered 1.2.2 through the update notice. Download
  and restart installs it and keeps the profile, plugins and settings.
- Installations of TypeWhisper 1.1 and 1.0 update to 1.2.2 directly, as described in
  the 1.2.0 release notes.
- Machine-wide installations are updated by deploying the 1.2.2 MSI.
- The Microsoft Store version is updated separately.

## Installation and requirements

- The per-user installer, the MSI and the portable ZIP are available for x64. Setup
  installs the .NET 10 runtime when it is missing; the portable ZIP requires it
  separately. The Group Policy templates are attached as
  `TypeWhisper-win-PolicyTemplates.zip`.
- TypeWhisper requires Windows 10 build 19041 or later.
- As with 1.2.0, the GitHub installers, including the MSI, are not code-signed, so
  Windows SmartScreen may ask for confirmation.
