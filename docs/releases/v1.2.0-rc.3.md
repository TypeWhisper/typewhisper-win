# TypeWhisper v1.2.0-rc.3

TypeWhisper 1.2.0-rc.3 is the third release candidate for Windows 1.2. The
[rc.1 release notes](https://github.com/TypeWhisper/typewhisper-win/releases/tag/v1.2.0-rc.1)
describe what is new in 1.2, including managed settings for organizations and the
machine-wide MSI.

## Changes since rc.2

- The Premium page is redesigned. Without Premium access it explains what a
  commercial license adds on Windows, with examples of what learning from corrections
  fixes, the entry price and a link to the plans. With access it lists the features
  with their status and settings.
- `TypeWhisper.exe --page <page>/<setting>` opens a settings page scrolled to one
  setting, for example `--page advanced/ManagedPolicy`. (#687)
- Settings search now also reaches the settings on the Audio page. (#687)

## Updating

- rc.2 offers rc.3 through the update notice. Download and restart installs it and
  keeps the profile, plugins and settings.
- Installations of TypeWhisper 1.1 on the release-candidate channel update to rc.3
  directly.
- Installations on the Stable channel stay on 1.1.0 until 1.2.0 is published.

## Installation and requirements

- The per-user installer, the MSI and the portable ZIP are available for x64. Setup
  installs the .NET 10 runtime when it is missing; the portable ZIP requires it
  separately. The Group Policy templates are attached as
  `TypeWhisper-win-PolicyTemplates.zip`.
- TypeWhisper requires Windows 10 build 19041 or later.
- As with rc.2, the GitHub installers, including the MSI, are not code-signed, so
  Windows SmartScreen may ask for confirmation. The Microsoft Store version is updated
  separately.

## RC validation notes

- Open Settings > Premium without a license and with one, in the light and dark theme.
- Run `TypeWhisper.exe --page advanced/ManagedPolicy` and check that Advanced opens at
  the managed settings card.
- Update rc.2 to rc.3 through the update notice.
